01 Principles

How a rule earns its place.

A linter that cries wolf gets switched off. Three constraints keep the signal high enough to run on every commit.

Proof, not pattern

A value is reported only after it decodes. A string that merely looks like an address and fails its checksum is SL014, not SL001.

Explainable

Every finding names the consequence in plain language and links a page with the specification reference. No opaque codes.

Suppressible

Vendored vectors and fixtures are a legitimate exception. One directive silences a line or a file, and it is visible in review.
